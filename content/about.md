I'm currently a tech lead at Veeam for the central platform engineering team.

## What I work on

I spend most of my time in the intersection of:

- **Platform & Infrastructure Engineering** — Kubernetes, cloud-native tooling, GitOps, IaC, Pulumi, etc.
- **Developer Experience** — internal platforms, CI/CD, making the boring stuff fast
- **Scalability** — I really like to design systems for scale -- I especially like to work on all the problems that scalability presents.
- **Operations** — I'm the one annoying engineer who keeps reminding managers to let our teams spend **more** time on operations (dashboards, alarms, metrics... you name it).'

## This blog

I write here to clarify my own thinking. Topics include:

- AI Platform engineering
- Cloud infrastructure and Kubernetes deep dives
- Engineering culture and how great teams work
- Rants about state of the art things

Posts tend to be technical and opinionated. I'd rather be specific and occasionally wrong than vague and always safe. These posts only represent **my view of the world, and they are not related to what my employer(s) think**.

Update: in October of 2026 I have removed most of my previous posts to start with a clean slate. I want this blog to be focused on platform engineering, K8s, etc. rather than lower level coding stuff.
